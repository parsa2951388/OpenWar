# Open War mobile and storefront polish

Date: 2026-11-06

## Goals
- Fit mobile navigation, username and clan-tag controls within narrow RTL screens.
- Remove the four oversized Classic information cards from the initial mobile viewport.
- Keep Help reachable while reducing the fixed top-bar footprint.
- Make the existing flags, skins, crowns and other cosmetics tabs usable on mobile.

## Source and pricing boundary
OpenBattle could not be loaded in this pass: direct HTTP and HTTPS attempts to the homepage and internal pages timed out. Consequently its current store catalog, exact amounts and currency could not be verified. Do not invent toman prices, copy unknown assets or alter payment amounts without an authoritative price source. Existing Open War catalog prices and payment paths remain authoritative until OpenBattle can be inspected.

## Acceptance checks
- No fixed-width logo/utility overlap on narrow phones.
- Clan-tag menu opens inward from the right edge and stays within viewport width.
- Classic landing no longer includes the four oversized feature cards.
- Help remains reachable from the mobile hamburger drawer.
- Cosmetics tabs fit a 320px viewport.
- Existing Classic lobby feed and New/Operations public gate remain intact.
